International Quantumn Online Exam

Advancing Electronics, Photonics, Light, and Quantum Mechanics, no age limits.

The exam is SO CHALLENGING! The International quantum online exam IQOE consists of 90 multiple-choice, mostly calculation based questions.Each question has four options (A, B, C, D), only one of which is correct. IQOE covers mainly 4 sections: Electronics, Phtonics, Light and Quantum Mechanics.
Rules:
_ You are required to send as much possible solutions as you can out of 90 questions in one hour.
_ No registration required, on May 20th we will publish the exam's sheet on this website, and you need to send your solutions via email.
_ After one hour we won't accept any submissions and results will be lauched in approximately a day or two.
_ There are 4 marks for each question. If you leave a question you don't lose marks. However, if you submit wrong answers you earn -4.
_ You have to send a One email including your answers. This should include:
Name. Institution. Country. Your answers.
_ Please know that any lack of the previous information or any unknown participation won't be corrected.
_ You can send your answers like this example: S1: 1 A, 3 D, 25 C for questions 1,3, 25 in section one.You should include the section S1, S2, S3 or S4.

The email-submission method adds a new challenge! Who can solve more questions and type their submission email within the 1 hour short time?!!
